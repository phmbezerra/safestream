SafeStream T-12 · VP8 Fan-out

One real screen-capture publisher → 2, 5, 10 or 20 independently authenticated WebRTC viewers in one browser tab. Measures decoded video, not RTP delivery alone.

Research limitations: Local loopback only; all decoders and publisher share one browser/computer. This is a combined SFU + client CPU stress test, not a measurement of independent viewer devices or internet capacity. VP8 is readable by the SFU (not E2EE). Share only non-sensitive test video. Keep this dashboard visible in the foreground during measurements.
