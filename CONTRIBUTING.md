# Contributing to SafeStream

Thanks for your interest in research that puts safety, privacy and empirical evidence first.

## Before contributing

This repository is in its documentation-first phase. A license and contributor policy have not yet been selected. Until then, please prefer discussions and issues over substantial code submissions; do not assume publication grants permission to reuse content.

## Contribution principles

1. Distinguish established evidence from hypotheses, design goals and speculation.
2. Reference primary sources for legal or regulatory claims and specify access dates.
3. Submit reproducible tests and openly document failures and limitations.
4. Use synthetic personas and safe test media; do not upload genuine abuse content, identifying minors' data, leaked credentials, or third-party private communications.
5. Do not test against Discord or any third-party production service without authorization.
6. Minimize personal data; do not add monitoring or recording by default.
7. Obtain review before proposing surveillance, age inference or content-classification features.

## Suggested workflow

- Open an issue describing a problem, expected behavior and safe reproduction steps.
- For implementation work after licensing is clarified, propose a narrow change and reference the issue.
- Include tests, security implications, documentation updates and known limitations.
- Request review and avoid asserting deployment or compliance readiness.

## Security issues

Do not publish a working exploit or credentials in a public issue. Until a private disclosure channel is configured, avoid sharing sensitive exploit details and request a private reporting method from maintainers.

## Documentation style

English, Markdown, descriptive section headings, explicit requirement IDs where relevant, and absolute dates for policy developments.

## Community

Treat affected people with dignity. No harassment, sensationalism, or demonstrations involving real people in distress. Academic critique, evidence-based disagreement and careful disclosure are encouraged.
